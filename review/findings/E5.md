### F1 · P1 · FACT · L211 · "Check Your Understanding"
> - B. In the slicer, add a pause at the layer just above the magnet pocket's floor, drop the magnets in when the printer stops, then let it print over them.

**Proposed:** - B. In the slicer, add a pause just before the first layer that covers the magnet pocket, drop the magnets in when the printer stops, then let it print over them.
**Why:** Pausing just above the floor leaves no pocket walls; nozzle hits the magnet. Contradicts L59.

### F2 · P1 · FACT · L218 · "Check Your Understanding"

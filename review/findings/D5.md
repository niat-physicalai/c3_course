### F6 · P2 · FACT · L92 · "Reading a Crash Report"
> 3. **`MEPC`** and the **backtrace**: the address of the instruction that failed, and the chain of calls that led there.

**Proposed:** 3. **`MEPC`** and the **stack dump**: `MEPC` is the address of the instruction that failed. The stack dump is raw memory from which a decoder rebuilds the chain of calls, called the backtrace.
**Why:** On the RISC-V ESP32-C3, the panic handler prints a stack dump. The decoder builds the backtrace from it.

### F13 · P2 · STRUCTURE · L366 · "Check Your Understanding" (Q4)
> **4.** Why start every log message with `#`?

**Proposed:** replace Q4 and its answer with:
**4.** Your D3 plotting script crashes when it reaches `[W] loop: motion sensor not answering`. What is the smallest fix that keeps the log message?

- A. Lower the baud rate.
- B. Start every log line with `#`, so the parser skips it.
- C. Set `LOG_LEVEL` to Debug.
- D. Remove the `[W]` tag.

Answer: **B.** D3's parser skips lines starting with `#`, so logs and data can share one serial port. **A** changes nothing about the content. **C** prints more log lines, not fewer. **D** leaves a line that is still not data.

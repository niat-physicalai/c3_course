### F13 · P2 · TIGHTEN · L101 · "The Questions, in Order"
> The last question changes decisions more than any other. A **false positive** reports something that did not happen: a step that was a clap. … Decide which matters more *before* choosing, because it tells you which sensor weakness you cannot accept.

**Proposed:** The last question changes decisions most. A **false positive** reports something that did not happen: a step that was really a clap. A **false negative** misses something that did happen: a heartbeat not detected. For a step counter, both are mild. For a fall detector, a missed fall is far worse than a false alarm. Decide which matters more *before* choosing: it tells you which sensor weakness you cannot accept.
**Why:** The reject-gate example repeats Worked Example 2's cap check. The fall detector returns in MCQ 1.
- [x] applied

### F4 · P1 · FACT · L128 · "Worked Example: Sizing the USB-C Opening"
> **Check.** Compare that distance with how far the plug's metal shell sticks out beyond its overmould. If the shell is shorter than 2.5 mm, the plug cannot fully seat, and the port will not charge reliably. … The numbers above are illustrative; the method is what matters.

**Proposed:** **Check.** An opening sized for the overmould lets the overmould enter the wall, so the plug seats. If the opening is smaller, the overmould stops at the outside of the wall. The metal shell must then cross these 2.5 mm *and* still go fully into the socket, which many cables cannot. Fixes: enlarge the opening, thin the wall locally, or move the socket closer to the board edge in C2.
**Why:** The shell needs travel plus full mating depth, not just 2.5 mm. The original contradicts Step 2.
- [x] applied

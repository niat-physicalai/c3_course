### F2 · P1 · FACT · L104 · "The Four Measurements"
> Why does the pitch error grow? Suppose a downloaded footprint uses 2.50 mm instead of 2.54 mm. The first pin is right, the second is 0.04 mm off, and the eighth is 7 × 0.04 = 0.28 mm off. A 1.0 mm hole with a 0.64 mm square pin has some slack, but not that much, and the error in the *other direction* at the far end of the row doubles the misfit.

**Proposed:** The pitch error grows along the row. Suppose a footprint uses 2.50 mm instead of 2.54 mm. Pin 1 is right, pin 2 is 0.04 mm off, and pin 8 is 7 × 0.04 = 0.28 mm off. A 0.64 mm square pin is about 0.9 mm across its corners, so a 1.0 mm hole leaves only about 0.05 mm of slack.
**Why:** With pin 1 aligned, all the error is in one direction. "Doubles the misfit" is wrong, and "not that much" is vague.
- [x] applied

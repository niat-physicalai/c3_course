**Overall intent:** a crash course for BTech students — take a problem statement, apply basic IoT
skills, and turn it into a real-life electronics/IoT product (sensors, microcontrollers, PCB, enclosure).

**Author decisions, 2026-09-28:**
- **Memory management** (Firmware column) — confirmed **out of scope**, too deep a concept for this course.
- **Rendering, material, colour, material selection** (3D Design column) — confirmed in scope; had no
  unit, now covered by the new **D1b — Materials, Colour & Rendering** in `CURRICULUM.md`.
- **Building a POC on breadboard, debugging hardware, PCB fabrication/assembly, 3D printing** — these
  stay out of *this* course by design. The author confirms they happen later, in the funded
  build/competition, when the student builds the physical product this course's design pack describes.
